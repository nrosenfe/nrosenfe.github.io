Time Series Analysis of MERS Infections

April 2024 · R · Seasonal AR · Partially observed Markov processes

Modeled MERS infection dynamics and examined seasonality and zoonotic transmission patterns.

Project overview

This project examines MERS infection dynamics using seasonal autoregressive models and partially observed Markov processes. The analysis considers transmission from animal to human populations and compares model fit with observed case data.
